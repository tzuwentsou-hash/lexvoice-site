Frequently asked questions

Recording does not start

Open Settings › LexVoice and make sure Microphone is on.

AI processing fails or I see "Pending Drafts"

The recording is saved on your iPhone when there is no connection. Open the draft on the Today tab and tap "Run AI on Saved Recording" once you are online.

How is time rounded?

Every entry is rounded up to the next 6 minutes (0.1 hour). For example, 13 minutes is billed as 0.3 hours. You can adjust hours by 0.1 in the review sheet.

Where is my data?

Your clients, matters and time entries are stored only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel them in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Use "Restore Purchases" on the upgrade screen on a new device.

Delete my data

LexVoice has no accounts. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your iPhone. Audio sent for processing is not retained by us.

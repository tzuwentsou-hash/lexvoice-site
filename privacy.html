Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026. Operator: Ziwen Zou ("we"). Contact: eztsou@gmail.com.

LexVoice is a time-entry formatting app for lawyers. We built it so that confidential client information stays on your device. We do not run a database of your clients or time entries, we have no user accounts, and we do not track you or show ads.

Data stored on your iPhone

Client and matter names, hourly rates, time entries, transcripts, billing narratives, your firm profile and settings are stored only on your device. Your optional own Gemini API key are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. We cannot see any of this.

Data sent for AI processing

When you stop a recording, use a Siri quick log, or run AI on a saved recording, the app sends the following to our gateway: the audio recording or dictated text, your list of active client and matter names with hourly rates (so entries can be matched), your billing mode, your language, and the timer's elapsed seconds. The gateway forwards this to Google's Gemini API to produce the billing entry and returns the result to your phone.

  • We do not store the audio, text or results. The gateway only logs operational data such as billing mode, model, latency and success, without content.
  • Requests to Gemini are sent with data storage disabled.
  • If you add your own Gemini API key in Settings, requests go through the gateway with your key and are subject to Google's terms for that key.
  • You agree to this processing on first launch. If you do not agree, do not use the AI features.

Recordings saved while offline remain on your device until you process or delete them. After successful processing, the recording file is deleted.

Anonymous device identifier

To apply a fair-use monthly limit, the app sends a random identifier generated on your device. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID or contacts, and is used only for rate limiting.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, authentication is handled by iOS. We do not receive biometric data.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or tracking. Beyond Google (for AI processing, as described above) we share data with no third parties.

Children

LexVoice is for legal professionals and is not directed at children.

Your choices

Because data lives on your device, you can edit or delete entries in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app. For any other request, email us.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
